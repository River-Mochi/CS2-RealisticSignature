# Realistic Signature Buildings

**Build freely without stacking unrealistic bonuses.**

A Cities: Skylines II mod by RiverMochi. Place multiple **Unique and Signature buildings**, and choose how their bonuses work. Version **0.8.1** extends bonus controls to eligible **non-Signature Unique buildings** too (for example, City Hall).

## Features and defaults

- **City-wide bonuses:** First copy only (default), Vanilla stacking, or Off.
- **City-wide Attractiveness:** Controlled separately — First copy only (default), Vanilla stacking, Off, or Follow city-wide.
- **Nearby effects:** Vanilla (default), First copy only, or Off. Abandoned-building local effects stay unchanged.
- **Multiple Unique copies:** ON by default, including Signature buildings, DLC, and compatible custom Unique assets.
- **Unlock Signature buildings:** Optional, OFF by default. Does not unlock other Unique buildings or missing DLC.
- **Cleaner UI:** Hide mass Signature unlock notifications and automatic Unique-building infoview overlays (both ON by default). Manual infoviews still work.

## Good to know

- Bonus controls affect buildings **with eligible bonuses**; not every Unique building has them.
- The game's green building bonus descriptions still show vanilla values. Check **Options → Actions → Status** or the log reports to see the mod's rules.
- Compatible with **Anarchy**. No Harmony patches or required third-party mods.
- Safe to remove: placed duplicates remain, and vanilla rules and bonuses return after reloading without the mod.
- Existing players may keep their saved settings instead of the new-install defaults.

Available in **15 languages**.
